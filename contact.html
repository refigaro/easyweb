Common questions before reaching out

Do I need to have a brief or know exactly what I want?
No. "I need a website for my plumbing business and my current one is embarrassing" is enough. We'll ask the right questions. You don't need to know anything about web design or what stack you want.
How long does a project take?
Business websites: 2–3 weeks. Online stores: 3–5 weeks. Landing pages: 5–7 business days. The biggest variable is how fast you provide feedback and content. We'll give you a timeline with your quote.
I already have a site. Can you redesign it instead of building from scratch?
Yes — most of our clients come to us with an existing site that isn't performing. A redesign is typically the same price as a new build. We migrate your content, fix what's slow or broken, and build something that actually converts. Share your URL in the form above and we'll take a look before we quote.
How does payment work?
50% to start, 50% on launch. You don't pay the second half until the site is live and you're satisfied. The Monthly Care Plan is billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice.
What if I don't like the design?
We show you the design before we write a single line of code. You approve it — or we revise it. Nothing gets built until you sign off. This is the checkpoint that eliminates the "I paid for something I hate" problem.
You're based in Mexico — is that a problem?
Easyweb is a US-registered LLC — the same legal standing as any American company you'd hire. You pay in USD, sign a US contract, and your IP is protected under USMCA. The team is in Mexico City, which means 6–8 hours of real-time overlap with US business hours. Most clients find communication is faster than with US agencies because there's no account manager relay.
