Pricing questions

Can I get a custom quote for something not in the plans?
Yes. The plans above cover the most common cases, but if you need something outside them — a larger store, a custom web app, a site migration, or a specific integration — reach out and we'll scope it out. We'll give you a written proposal with a fixed price before anything starts.
Why 50% upfront? What does the other 50% cover?
The 50% deposit secures your spot and covers the design phase. The remaining 50% is due when the site launches — meaning you don't pay the second half until you've seen and approved the finished product on a staging site. This protects both parties: you don't pay in full for something you haven't seen, and we're not working for free.
What if I need more than 5 pages?
Additional pages beyond the plan limit are $75 each. This covers design and development for the extra page — using the same design system as the rest of your site, so everything stays consistent.
Do you offer refunds?
The deposit is non-refundable once design work starts — it covers the time invested in discovery and Figma design. If we haven't started yet and you need to cancel, we'll work something out. The second payment (50% on launch) is only due once you've approved the site — if you don't approve it, we keep iterating until you do.
What does the Care Plan's 12 hours of content changes actually cover?
Updating text, swapping photos, adding or removing services, updating business hours, adding a new team member, publishing a blog post you've written — anything that's a content change rather than a structural redesign. Most months clients use between 0 and 2 hours. The 12-hour ceiling is there for busy months, not as a target.
Can I cancel the Care Plan or Hosting at any time?
The Care Plan is month-to-month — cancel anytime with 30 days notice. If you cancel hosting, we'll help you migrate your site to a hosting provider of your choice. You own the site and all its files regardless of whether you stay with our hosting or not.
