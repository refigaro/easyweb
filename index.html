Common questions

The things people ask before deciding to work with us.

You're in Mexico — how does that affect my project?
Easyweb is a US-registered LLC. The team is in Mexico, which is how we keep prices low. In practice, you won't notice a difference: we respond in English, we're on Slack and Zoom during your business hours (CST — 6 to 8 hours of overlap with US time zones), and your contract, payments, and IP transfer are all handled under US law. Intellectual property is protected under USMCA.
What if I don't like the design?
We don't write a single line of code until you approve the design. We work in Figma — you can see the full layout, colors, and typography before anything is built. Every plan includes revision rounds at the design stage so we get it right before development starts. This is the most common source of disputes with web agencies, and our process specifically eliminates it.
I already have a website. Can you redesign it instead of starting fresh?
Yes — and this is actually our most common project type. We audit your current site first: what's working, what's hurting your rankings or conversions, and what needs to change. Then we rebuild only what needs rebuilding. You don't lose your domain authority or existing content unless it should go.
Do I pay monthly, or is it a one-time cost?
The design and build is a one-time payment — you own the site 100%. For the recurring part, you have two options: Hosting + Domain at $85/year (we host your site on our enterprise plan and manage your domain — SSL included, no dashboards to deal with), or the Monthly Care Plan at $99/month, which includes hosting plus backups, security monitoring, and up to 12 hours of content changes per month. You can also handle hosting yourself if you prefer — we'll point you to the right setup.
Can I update the site myself after launch?
Yes. Every site is built with a CMS so you can update text, swap photos, and add content without touching code. We include a walkthrough session after launch so you're fully independent. If you'd rather not deal with it, that's what the Monthly Care Plan is for.
What happens after my site launches?
You keep my contact. If something breaks or needs a quick fix, I respond within 1 business day. The Monthly Care Plan formalizes ongoing support — but either way, you won't hit a wall the week after launch. That "they disappeared after launch" story is the most common complaint in web design. Our process is designed around making sure it doesn't happen.
