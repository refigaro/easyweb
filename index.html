FAQ

Common questions

The things people ask before deciding to work with us.

You're in Mexico — how does that affect my project?

Easyweb is a US-registered LLC. The team is in Mexico, which is how we keep prices low. In practice, you won't notice a difference: we respond in English, we're on Slack and Zoom during your business hours (CST — 6 to 8 hours of overlap), and your contract, payments, and IP transfer are all handled under US law.

What if I don't like the design?

We don't write a single line of code until you approve the design. We work in Figma — you can see the full layout, colors, and typography before anything is built. Every plan includes revision rounds at the design stage.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it instead of starting fresh?

Yes — and this is actually our most common project type. We audit your current site first, then rebuild only what needs rebuilding. You don't lose your domain authority or existing content unless it should go.

Do I pay monthly, or is it a one-time cost?

The design and build is a one-time payment — you own the site 100%. Optional recurring: Hosting + Domain at $85/year or the Monthly Care Plan at $99/month (hosting + backups + security + 12 hrs/month content changes).

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. Every site is built with a CMS so you can update text, swap photos, and add content without touching code. We include a walkthrough session after launch so you're fully independent.

What happens after my site launches?

You keep my contact. If something breaks or needs a quick fix, I respond within 1 business day. The Monthly Care Plan formalizes ongoing support — but either way, you won't hit a wall the week after launch.
