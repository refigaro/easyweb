What a bad website actually costs you

A website that looks unprofessional or loads slowly doesn't just fail to help — it actively loses you customers. Studies consistently show that 75% of people judge a business's credibility based on its website. If your site looks like it was built in 2015, that's the impression you're leaving.

Speed matters just as much. Google penalizes slow sites in search rankings, and users abandon pages that take more than 3 seconds to load on mobile. If your site isn't fast, it's invisible — both to Google and to the customer who doesn't wait.

The fix isn't complicated, but it requires doing it right from the start. A fast, professional, SEO-ready site isn't a luxury for small businesses — it's the baseline for being taken seriously online.

Common concerns

What if I don't have photos or copy ready?
You don't need to have everything ready before we start. We design first, then fill in content. If you don't have photos, we use high-quality royalty-free images at no extra cost. For copy, we can work with bullet points you give us and write the final text — or if you prefer, you write it and we apply it. Either way works.
What if I already have a site and just want to improve it?
We start with an audit of your current site — what's working, what's hurting your rankings, what's causing visitors to leave. Then we rebuild only what needs to change. You don't lose your domain, your content, or your Google authority unnecessarily.
14 business days feels fast — can the quality really be that good?
The 14-day timeline assumes you're responsive — you review designs within 24 hours and provide feedback promptly. If there are delays on your end, the timeline extends accordingly. The speed comes from having a defined process: discovery → design → approval → build → staging → launch. No scope creep, no rework from late-stage changes.
What happens if I need changes after the site launches?
Small content changes (text, photos, adding a service) you can do yourself through the CMS. For anything bigger, the Monthly Care Plan ($99/mo) includes up to 12 hours of changes per month. Or you can request individual changes at our hourly rate — we'll quote you before doing anything.
