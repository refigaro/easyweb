The problem

What happens to your site
when no one's watching it

A business website isn't a one-time project. It's infrastructure. And infrastructure breaks when it's not maintained.

It goes down

Hosting expires, a plugin update breaks something, a server restarts. Without monitoring, you find out when a customer tells you.

It gets hacked

Outdated plugins are the #1 attack vector for WordPress sites. A hacked site loses Google rankings — sometimes permanently.

You can't change it

New service to add. Price change. Team member update. Without a developer on call, small things pile up for months.

It slows down

No cache optimization, no image compression, no performance review. Six months later your fast site is slow — and you're losing SEO.

The Care Plan handles all of this. So you never have to think about it.

Updating text, swapping photos, adding or removing services, changing prices, adding a team member, publishing a blog post you've written, updating business hours, fixing a broken link. Common questions

Does the Care Plan work for sites Easyweb didn't build?
Yes — as long as your site is on WordPress. We'll do a brief technical audit (free, 1 business day) to confirm the site is in good enough shape to maintain. If it needs cleanup before we can take it on, we'll quote that separately and be upfront about what it costs.
What if I use more than 12 hours in a month?
We'll let you know before going over. You can approve additional hours at our standard rate, or we can roll the work into the next month. We never go over budget without your explicit approval — and in practice, most clients use 2–4 hours per month.
Can I cancel the Care Plan at any time?
Yes. Month-to-month, cancel with 30 days notice. If you cancel, we'll help you migrate your site to a hosting provider of your choice and hand over all credentials and documentation. You own the site — we're just managing it.
Is this different from just paying a freelancer for updates?
Significantly. A freelancer handles requests when you send them — they're not watching your site. The Care Plan includes proactive monitoring, daily backups, and security patching that happens whether you ask for it or not. And when something breaks at 11pm, there's someone to respond — not a freelancer you might not hear back from until tomorrow.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
No. Month-to-month, cancel anytime. We're confident enough in the service that we don't need to lock you in. A simple service agreement covers the scope and terms — same as a standard MSA, nothing unusual.
