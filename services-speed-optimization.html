Common questions

Is one page really enough to get customers?
For many service businesses, yes. If someone finds you through Google, a referral, or a social ad and your page answers three questions — "What do they do?", "Can I trust them?", "How do I contact them?" — they'll reach out. A single focused page that answers those questions clearly outperforms a bloated five-page site that buries the point.
Can I use this as a Google Ads landing page?
Yes — and if that's the purpose, tell us upfront. A conversion-focused landing page for ads is built differently than a general landing page: no navigation links to distract visitors, a single clear CTA, and a structure designed to match the ad's message. We'll design it accordingly.
What's the difference between this and a DIY builder like Wix or Squarespace?
Three main differences: (1) Custom design — yours is built from scratch, not picked from a template library shared by thousands of other businesses. (2) Performance — DIY builder pages are typically slower and harder to optimize for PageSpeed. (3) You own it — there's no platform dependency. If Wix shuts down a plan, your site goes with it. WordPress sites are yours regardless of what any platform does.
