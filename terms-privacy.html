Collected data is used exclusively to:

4. Service terms

By contracting any Easyweb service (Landing Page, Business Website, Online Store, Hosting + Domain, or Monthly Care Plan), the client agrees to the following:

  • Scope: The project scope, deliverables, price, and timeline are defined in a written proposal and Master Service Agreement (MSA) signed before work begins.
  • Payment: Project plans require a 50% deposit to start and 50% on launch. Hosting and Care Plan services are billed annually or monthly as specified.
  • Revisions: Each plan includes a defined number of design revision rounds before development begins. Revisions requested after development starts may incur additional charges.
  • Timeline: Delivery timelines assume the client reviews and provides feedback within 24 business hours at each checkpoint. Delays on the client's side extend the timeline accordingly.
  • Cancellation: The deposit is non-refundable once design work has started. Monthly Care Plan subscriptions may be cancelled with 30 days written notice.
